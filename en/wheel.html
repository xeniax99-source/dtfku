How to use the online prayer wheel

This short guide explains how to choose the number of turns, read the counter, start the test animation, and manage a wish.

NUMBER OF TURNS · LOCAL ANIMATION

Choose a number of turns.
The counter records completed turns.

On the home page, choose a preset — 7, 21, 108, or 1,008 turns — or enter a custom number from 1 to 5,000. The running total on this device increases only when a full turn is completed.

If Reduce Motion is enabled in your device settings, the animation performs no more than ten turns. The displayed test amount is still based on your selected number; no payment is made.

STEP BY STEP · ONLINE PRACTICE

Set an intention.
Choose a number.
Start the wheel.

That is all the interface needs: an optional wish, a turn count, and your start. You may recite “Om mani padme hum” or spend this pause in silence.

01

A brief intention

Write a wish of up to 200 characters, or keep it in mind; the field is optional.

02

Choose a number

Choose one of the four presets or enter any number from 1 to 5,000. Confirm to start the animation; the amount shown is for testing only.

03

Watch and dedicate

Watch the animation, recite a mantra, or simply stay with your intention. The counter adds completed turns; at the end, you may dedicate the good wish to the well-being of all beings.

PRIVACY · TEST MODE

Your wish stays with you.
No real charges are made.

Your wish is not saved automatically. It is added to a journal in this browser on this device only if you actively tick the separate consent box. Entries are not sent to a server and can be deleted.

The amount shown is for demonstration only: no payment provider is connected, no money is charged, and bank-card details are neither requested nor collected.

The online animation is a contemporary digital format; it does not replace a physical mani wheel or the guidance of a particular Buddhist community.
